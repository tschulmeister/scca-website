Based on an analysis of the provided financial spreadsheets spanning 2020 through mid-2026, here is an overview of the **Shipley's Choice Community Association (SCCA)** financial condition, income trends, and expenditure profiles.

---

### 1. Dues Participation & Revenue Trends

* **Declining Participation:** Community dues participation has seen a steady downward trend over recent years:
* **2020:** 209 of 283 households paid (~73.9%).
* **2022:** 215 of 283 households paid (75.7% / 76%).
* **2023:** 210 of 283 households paid (73.9% / 74%).
* **2024:** 205 of 283 households paid (72.2% / 72%).
* **2025:** 196 of 283 households paid (69.0% / 69%).
* **2026 (as of June 4, 2026):** 194 of 283 households paid (68.3% / 69%).


* **Budgeted vs. Actual Dues Revenue:**
* Proposed budgets for 2022 and 2023 assumed a target of 250 paying homes at $75 per year, projecting **$18,750** in annual revenue.
* Actual revenue has consistently fallen short of the 250-household assumption due to participation settling around 194–215 households (~$14,550 to $16,125 at $75/home).

### 2. Major Expense Categories & Cost Drivers

Across all years, SCCA’s expenses are dominated by a few recurring categories:

1. **Tree Removal & Maintenance (Largest Expense):**
* **2020:** $7,550 actual YTD (plus $600–$1,000 in upcoming/potential expenses early 2021).
* **2022 & 2023 Budgets:** Allocated **$12,000** annually.
* **2026 Budget:** Allocated **$8,000**.


2. **Insurance Premium Increases:**
* **2020 Actual:** $1,851.00.
* **2022 & 2023 Budgets:** $1,700.00 budgeted.
* **2026 Actual (YTD June):** $4,279.00 actual vs. $3,850.00 budgeted. Insurance costs have more than doubled since 2020.


3. **Lawn Maintenance & Groundskeeping:**
* **2020 Actual:** $2,770.96.
* **2022 & 2023 Budgets:** $1,500.00 (12 treatments).
* **2026 Budget:** $3,500.00 ($845.00 spent as of June 4, 2026).


4. **Covenant Renewal / Special Projects (Non-recurring):**
* Section 2 Covenant Renewals were budgeted at $2,000 in 2022 and 2023.

### Key Takeaways & Recommendations

1. **Dues Participation Erosion:** Participation has dropped from ~76% in 2022 to ~68% in 2026 (a loss of 21 paying households). Re-engaging non-paying households or adjusting dues rates may be required to maintain budget solvency.
2. **Rising Fixed Costs:** Insurance costs have surged to $4,279 in 2026 (exceeding its $3,850 budget line item and up over 130% from 2020 levels).
3. **Tree Removal Exposure:** Tree removal is consistently the single largest operational variable cost, taking up $8,000–$12,000 annually.
4. **Liquidity Buffer:** SCCA maintains a stable baseline cash balance around $6,700–$8,200, but timing of major tree removal work relative to annual dues collection is critical to avoiding short-term liquidity squeezes.
