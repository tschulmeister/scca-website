# FAQ content proposed by board member:

Q. What financial safeguards are there?
A.  The laws for SCBD’s require strong financial accountability.  Some of the mandated protections are:
    • It is illegal to co-mingle Community Association funds and SCBD funds.  Each entity is required to have their own checking accounts, keep separate books, etc.
    • The use of SCBD funds for any purpose other than those explicitly listed in the petition are prohibited.
    • The treasurer and other officers are required be bonded.  Certificates from the bonding company must be sent directly from the insurance company to the A.A. Co. Finance Office.
    • Formal financial reports and other special reporting must be submitted to the A.A. Co. Finance multiple times per year.
    • The SCBD must adhere to several other standards regarding accounting practices, record keeping, communications, etc. 
    • Based on our size, the SBCD is required to have audits by an independent Certified Public Accountant at least once every four years.
    • The “administrating entity” (i.e., the Shipley’s Choice Community Association, Inc.) is required to have a Certificate of Good Standing issued by the Maryland Department of Assessments and Taxation and to keep it current.
    • The SCBD is not permitted to operate at a deficit in any given year.  The A.A. Office of Finance will not distribute any funds that would exceed the approved annual budget of the SCBD.

Q. How much is the assessment expected to be?
A. The precise amount cannot be determined until the SCBD is prepared and approved.  However, the board’s preliminary estimate is that the SCBD assessment will be about $70 per year per household and will be included in your annual property tax bill.  Membership in the SCCA will continue to voluntary and open to all Shipley’s Choice residents.  Dues will be reduced and may be in the vicinity $10 to $15 annually.

Q. Are there any SCCA expenditures not covered by SCBD?
A. Yes. Petitions for SCBD’s are not permitted to include expenses related what are considered routine civic association functions.  Examples are community newsletters, websites, memberships (e.g. GSPC), social events, lobbying or advocating (such as opposing or supporting nearby zoning changes).  Such expenses, if any, must be borne by the administering entity (SCCA).



 

#FAQ content used by another board member for general SCBDs:

Where can I learn more about Special Community Benefit Districts and Codes?

The law concerning Special Community Benefit Districts appears in Article 4, Title 7 of the Anne Arundel County Code. The Code is available on the County’s website at www.aacounty.org.  The Office of the Budget has developed administrative guidelines to implement the law. The following guidelines include the process for establishing and operating a Special Community Benefit District.

 

Will the budget be made available before the annual SCCA meeting each year and if so, how?

Yes. The budget will be made publicly available via mail and on the SCCA website in advance of the annual meeting.

 

When is the vote going to be on this proposal?

The vote will not take place on a set date, as 2/3 of homeowners are needed in order for the vote to go through.  The petition will be sent out with the annual newsletter and ballots and collection of votes will begin then. 

 

How will the tax be applied? Is it a lump sum per household?

The tax will be included with your property tax bill, but shown as a separate line item.  The tax will be applied as a uniform assessment per tax account.

 

Who would be responsible for our Special Community Benefit District?

The Shipley’s Choice Community Association (SCCA) Board is responsible for working within the County’s guidelines and laws on all expenditures related to money collected under our SCBD petition.  Each year the SCCA Board and SCBD Committee creates a proposed budget for the coming year and sets a proposed tax rate for the SCBD based on anticipated needs. The Budget is published in the newsletter and sent to all members of SCCA

Who tallies the petition votes and verifies their legitimacy?

The signed petitions are sent to the County for verification. The Special Taxing District Coordinator reviews the petitions and will determine if each petition is valid.

 

How many votes does each resident get regarding the petition? Who is required to sign the petition?

An owner gets 1 “vote” per improved property. According to Section 4-7-101(4), both a “husband and wife” owner must sign the petition in order for their vote to count.  A “partnership” must also have 2 signatures. A “legal entity” owner is only required to obtain 1 signature from a legal representative. In the case of “multiple ownership” again only 1 signature of a legal representative is required. Each of the entities described above will register 1 vote if cast.

 

If your improved property is held in a trust, please submit a copy of the first page of the trust and a copy of the signature page of the trust in addition to signing the petition.

 

If someone on the deed is deceased, the county requires a copy of their death certificate in addition to a signed petition which should be signed in accordance with Section 4-7-101(4) referenced above.

 

 

What types of uses can the funds be use for? What can they NOT be used for?

Funds can only be used for purposes as listed on the current petitions. The petitions states “the purposes of the district are:

1. To maintain and improve community-owned real and personal property, including open space, recreation property, and entry signs for Sections 1 and 2 of the community; and

2. To pay administrative expenses necessary to accomplish this purpose, including repayment of any loan and interest thereon, and payment of insurance, legal, utility, and administrative expenses. 

Funds CANNOT be used for purposes not listed on the petition. Each annual budget “shall provide a detailed listing of the purposes to which the funds are applied […]” The use of funds, as part of the budget, must be determined before-hand and approved on an annual basis.
