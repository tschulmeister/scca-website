The street light upgrade in Sections 1 and 2 of Shipley's Choice is underway! The light fixtures and poles are being upgraded in phases.

1 Section 1

The County has not confirmed the installation date for Section 1 yet.

2 Section 2

New light fixtures (not poles) were installed this week. Poles will be replaced in January, weather permitting.

You may notice the new fixtures project brighter light than the ones they replaced. The County's street light program manager asked SCCA to inform Shipley's homeowners that the new fixtures use the lowest wattage and best color temperature available; the brightness cannot be adjusted.

While the brighter lights may take some getting used to, the upgrade will improve the safety of our neighborhood.
