The Shipley's Choice Community Association (SCCA) Board of Directors has officially confirmed the date, time, and location for the 2026 Annual Membership Meeting. All residents of Sections 1 and 2 are highly encouraged to attend this pivotal community session.

Meeting Logistics

  • Date & Time: Tuesday, October 20, 2026, at 7:00 PM
  • Location: Shipley's Choice Swim and Tennis Club Clubhouse (947 Rustling Oaks Dr, Millersville, MD 21108)

Key Agenda & SCCA Bylaw Context

As outlined in our official Bylaws (Articles V & VI), the October Annual Meeting is the most significant democratic forum of our community's fiscal year. At this meeting, we conduct general elections for SCCA Officers and Directors, present a comprehensive review of SCCA's financial health, discuss ongoing maintenance initiatives, and provide updates regarding our petition to designate SCCA as a Special Community Benefits District (SCBD).

Please keep the following essential voting and governance parameters in mind as we approach the meeting:

Voting Rights & Membership

Per Article V, Section 4 of the bylaws, each household in Sections 1 and 2 is restricted to exactly two (2) voting privileges, which must be exercised by qualified adult members in good standing (meaning annual dues are current). Dues for the 2026–2027 fiscal year become due and payable on October 1st.

Establishment of Quorum

Under Article V, Section 4, a quorum of twenty-five percent (25%) of voting members is legally required to conduct association business. Meeting this threshold is vital to ensure our general elections and motions are valid under Maryland law.

Meeting Schedule & Order of Business

In accordance with Article V, Section 5, the meeting will be conducted by the chair following established Robert's Rules of Parliamentary Procedure. Under Article X, the official agenda and flow of the session will strictly observe the following designated order of business:

  1. 1. Reading of the Minutes of the previous regular meeting.
  2. 2. Report of the Board of Directors.
  3. 3. Report of the Treasurer (and Special Auditing Committee review).
  4. 4. Correspondence.
  5. 5. Special Committees & Election of SCCA Officers and Directors.
  6. 6. Report of Special Committees.
  7. 7. Report of Standing Committees.
  8. 8. Unfinished Business (including SCBD updates).
  9. 9. New Business (community floor suggestions and motions).
  10. 10. Adjournment.

Elections & Absentee Balloting

Under Article VI, Section 2, the general elections of Officers and Directors (who serve staggered two-year terms) will be finalized during the October meeting. To ensure full community participation:

  • Notification & Ballots: No later than two weeks prior to the October meeting (by October 6, 2026), the Secretary will mail official candidate selections along with absentee ballots to all household members.
  • Absentee Voting Counts: If you are unable to attend the meeting in person, please return your official absentee ballot to the Secretary. All returned absentee ballots are legally counted toward establishing our 25% quorum.
  • Confidentiality: Absentee ballot counts will be kept strictly secret until the written votes of members present at the meeting are counted on October 20.
  • Floor Nominations: In accordance with our democratic procedures, additional nominations for open Board vacancies may also be made directly from the floor prior to the start of the election.

Your voice and participation are what keep our neighborhood vibrant, beautiful, and safe. Please mark your calendars for October 20th at 7:00 PM, and stay tuned for your official election package in early October!
