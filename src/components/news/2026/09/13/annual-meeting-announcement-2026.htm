The Shipley's Choice Community Association (SCCA) Board of Directors has officially confirmed the date, time, and location for the 2026 Annual Membership Meeting. All residents of Sections 1 and 2 are highly encouraged to attend this pivotal community session.

Meeting Logistics

  • Date & Time: Tuesday, October 20, 2026, at 7:00 PM
  • Location: Shipley's Choice Swim and Tennis Club Clubhouse (947 Rustling Oaks Dr, Millersville, MD 21108)

Key Agenda & SCCA Bylaw Context

As outlined in our official Bylaws (Articles V & VI), the October Annual Meeting is the most significant democratic forum of our community's fiscal year. At this meeting, we conduct general elections for SCCA Officers and Directors, present a comprehensive review of SCCA's financial health, discuss ongoing maintenance initiatives, and provide updates regarding our petition to designate SCCA as a Special Community Benefits District (SCBD).

Please keep the following essential voting and governance parameters in mind as we approach the meeting:

Voting Rights & Membership

Per Article V, Section 4 of the bylaws, each household in Sections 1 and 2 is restricted to exactly two (2) voting privileges, which must be exercised by qualified adult members in good standing (meaning annual dues are current). Dues for the 2026–2027 fiscal year become due and payable on October 1st.

Establishment of Quorum

Under Article V, Section 4, a quorum of twenty-five percent (25%) of voting members is legally required to conduct association business. Meeting this threshold is vital to ensure our general elections and motions are valid under Maryland law.

Meeting Schedule & Order of Business

In accordance with Article V, Section 5, the meeting will be conducted by the chair following established Robert's Rules of Parliamentary Procedure. Under Article X, the official agenda and flow of the session will strictly observe the following designated order of business, detailed here with additional context and description for each segment:

  1. 1.
    Reading of the Minutes of the previous regular meeting The Secretary will present the official minutes from the 2025 Annual Membership Meeting. This ensures a transparent, legally-sound historical record is maintained, allowing voting members to review, verify, and formally approve the recorded resolutions and discussions before proceeding with new business.
  2. 2.
    Report of the Board of Directors The SCCA President and Directors will deliver a comprehensive overview of the board’s activities, milestones, and administrative highlights over the past fiscal year. This report summarizes our efforts to maintain neighborhood standards, coordinate community-wide communications, and protect our shared values.
  3. 3.
    Report of the Treasurer (and Special Auditing Committee review) Our Treasurer will present a detailed financial status report outlining SCCA's actual expenditures, revenues, and reserve fund status. This critical review will include the formal findings and certification from our resident-led Special Auditing Committee, verifying all financial ledgers are accurate and complete.
  4. 4.
    Correspondence The Secretary will read and address official written communications received by the SCCA Board from residents, local government bodies, or external partners during the year that warrant formal entry into the association’s official records.
  5. 5.
    Election of SCCA Officers and Directors This designates the formal opening of SCCA's democratic voting process. The Nominating Committee will present the official candidate roster for open Officer and Director positions. Eligible members will cast physical ballots, and verified absentee ballots will be legally compiled.
  6. 6.
    Report of Special Committees Representatives from any ad-hoc or temporary task forces appointed by the Board during the year will share progress updates, data analyses, and strategic recommendations developed to address specific, time-sensitive community projects.
  7. 7.
    Report of Standing Committees Reports from our established, permanent groups—such as the Architectural Review Committee (ARC) and the Grounds & Common Area Maintenance Committee—will highlight architectural compliance statistics, common-area preservation efforts, and upcoming landscaping initiatives.
  8. 8.
    Unfinished Business (including SCBD updates) The floor will open to discuss pending items tabled or left unresolved from prior sessions. This year, a major focus under unfinished business will be providing a status report and discussing the next steps on our petition to designate SCCA as a Special Community Benefits District (SCBD).
  9. 9.
    New Business (community floor suggestions and motions) Residents are highly encouraged to introduce fresh motions, propose community improvement suggestions, and ask open-floor questions. This segment serves as the primary forum for homeowners to directly introduce new initiatives for SCCA's active consideration.
  10. 10.
    Adjournment Once all scheduled items on the agenda have been fully addressed, floor discussions are concluded, and election results are officially verified and recorded, a formal motion to close the annual membership meeting will be made to adjourn.

Elections & Absentee Balloting

Under Article VI, Section 2, the general elections of Officers and Directors (who serve staggered two-year terms) will be finalized during the October meeting. To ensure full community participation:

  • Notification & Ballots: No later than two weeks prior to the October meeting (by October 6, 2026), the Secretary will mail official candidate selections along with absentee ballots to all household members.
  • Absentee Voting Counts: If you are unable to attend the meeting in person, please return your official absentee ballot to the Secretary. All returned absentee ballots are legally counted toward establishing our 25% quorum.
  • Confidentiality: Absentee ballot counts will be kept strictly secret until the written votes of members present at the meeting are counted on October 20.
  • Floor Nominations: In accordance with our democratic procedures, additional nominations for open Board vacancies may also be made directly from the floor prior to the start of the election.

Your voice and participation are what keep our neighborhood vibrant, beautiful, and safe. Please mark your calendars for October 20th at 7:00 PM, and stay tuned for your official election package in early October!

Clarification Regarding Association Governance & Receivership

Recent questions have been raised regarding county intervention or "receivership" in the event of vacant Board positions following our Annual Meeting. We would like to provide clear facts regarding Maryland law and our Association’s governing structure:

1 Receivership Does Not Apply to Voluntary Associations

Under Maryland law, statutory receivership is a court remedy specifically designed for mandatory HOAs and Condominium Associations that own and maintain shared real property infrastructure (such as private roads, common buildings, or stormwater management systems). Because the Shipley’s Choice Community Association (SCCA) is a voluntary civic association and not a mandatory HOA, these statutory receivership mechanisms simply do not apply to us.

2 The Board Has Legal Authority to Fill Vacancies

Under Article VI, Section 3 of our By-Laws and Maryland corporate law, our sitting Board of Directors retains full authority to appoint qualified community members in good standing to fill any vacant seats. Even if an annual meeting does not achieve the 25% voting quorum required for general elections, the Board has the explicit authority to fill open positions and maintain continuous governance.

3 SCCA Operations Remain Stable and Sound

Judicial receivership is an extraordinary remedy reserved strictly for catastrophic corporate collapse where zero leadership exists to manage assets. The SCCA is financially sound, operational, fully insured, and completely capable of maintaining its administrative and community responsibilities.
