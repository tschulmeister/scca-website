The SCCA Board is pleased to announce that registration is now officially open for residents wishing to attend the upcoming 2026 Annual Membership Meeting virtually on Tuesday, October 20, 2026, at 7:00 PM.

To ensure every resident has the opportunity to stay informed and engage with our neighborhood's governance, SCCA is providing a private virtual stream via Google Meet. If you are unable to join us in person at the Swim and Tennis Club Clubhouse, you can request access via our new online registration form. Private invite links will be sent out to verified residents 24 to 48 hours prior to the meeting.

Register for the Virtual Meeting

Why Sign Up for Virtual Attendance?

The Annual Membership Meeting is the most critical event of our fiscal year, covering elections for officers and directors, financial statements, and ongoing common grounds projects. Virtual registration enables you to:

  • Observe all Board presentations and reports live from your home.
  • Listen to the comprehensive Treasurer's financial update and SCCA audit certification.
  • Follow the floor discussions, project reviews, and SCBD petition updates in real-time.

Important Rules for Virtual Participants

Please note the SCCA voting and attendance rules established to comply with our community Bylaws and Maryland legal requirements:

Absentee Ballots Required

Binding Votes via Official Absentee Ballots Only: Because verifying identity and household eligibility in real-time for virtual attendees is difficult, binding votes cannot be cast verbally or via chat inside the Google Meet session. Virtual attendees who wish to vote must submit an official paper absentee ballot prior to the meeting cutoff.

Quorum Participation

Absentee Ballots Count Toward Quorum: Submitted absentee ballots legally count toward meeting the required 25% household quorum, whether the resident attends virtually or not.

View-Only Format

Virtual Attendance as View-Only / Floor Discussion: The Google Meet stream allows remote residents to view board presentations, hear financial/committee reports, and observe floor discussions in real time.

Households Limit

Two Votes Per Household Limit: Per Article V, Section 4 of the bylaws, each household in good standing is strictly entitled to two (2) total votes, which are validated against the official membership roster when paper or absentee ballots are submitted.

Official paper absentee ballots and detailed voting instructions will be distributed to all resident households in the coming weeks. If you plan to attend virtually, please make sure to fill out and return your ballot in advance to ensure your voice is counted and SCCA achieves its quorum.
