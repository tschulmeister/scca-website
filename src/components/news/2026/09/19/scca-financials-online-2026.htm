We are thrilled to announce that the official SCCA Statement of Financial Condition is now available directly online! Residents can now review SCCA's verified accounts, current operational expenses, and multi-year funding metrics under our new dedicated Financial Condition dashboard.

First and foremost, we would like to sincerely apologize for the wait in getting these financial documents posted online. As many of you know, the SCCA website and its operations are a volunteer labor of love. Over the past several months, our board webmaster has been executing a total, ground-up overhaul of our digital presence—migrating our entire ecosystem away from the legacy Weebly platform.

Building a modern, highly-featured, and secure portal from scratch has taken substantial time, as we wanted to ensure that every available historical document, map scan, meeting notes, and community resource list has been properly formatted, verified, and safely migrated. We deeply appreciate your patience while we did the hard work necessary to get our materials completely and accurately integrated.

The great news? This total redesign has been successfully completed, and by migrating our infrastructure from Weebly to Vercel, the SCCA now operates its entire state-of-the-art website at $0.00 ongoing operational hosting cost. Every dollar we save on administrative software overhead is a dollar that goes directly back into maintaining our commons areas, managing our tree safety, and protecting our community.

What's Included in the Financials Dashboard?

Our new Financials page has been built with transparency and user interaction in mind. When you visit the dashboard, you can:

  • Toggle Archived Statements: Seamlessly switch between the newly compiled September 19, 2026 Statement and the prior June 4, 2026 Statement to track financial movements.
  • Consolidated Balance Grid: View unencumbered cash positions, gross checking accounts, restricted playground funds, and outstanding checks to the penny.
  • Interactive Budget Progress: Track line-item YTD actual expenditures (such as tree removals and landscaping) against SCCA's approved annual operating budget with progress gauges.
  • Voluntary Dues & Burden Simulator: Adjust a slider to model community participation and see firsthand how the current voluntary system (~69% participation) impacts SCCA finances and places a higher effective financial burden on contributing properties compared to a unified SCBD model.
  • Raw Excel Ledger Downloads: Download the raw, verified spreadsheet files prepared directly by the SCCA Treasurer for total independent community oversight.

We encourage all residents to explore the new dashboard and download the verified files. Promoting transparency, protecting the integrity of our expenditures, and keeping our neighbors fully informed of SCCA operations remains a core priority of the Board.
