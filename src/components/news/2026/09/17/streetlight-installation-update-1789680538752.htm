We have received a major update from Anne Arundel County's Traffic Engineering Division regarding our community's streetlight replacement initiative. The county is actively moving forward with replacing our 45-year-old aging streetlights with modern, energy-efficient LED fixtures and brand-new poles!

Due to the scale of the upgrade and supply chain availability, the county will complete the installations in phases. They have provided the following scheduled timelines and details for our community's primary sectors:

1 Severncrest Area

The installation for Severncrest will occur in two sequential parts:

  • Fixtures (LED): Scheduled for replacement in October 2026.
  • Poles: Currently on backorder, with expected delivery in the December 2026 / January 2027 timeframe.

2 Rustling Oaks Dr Area

The County is currently coordinating scheduling with BGE:

  • Fixtures (LED): Expected around the end of the year (late 2026).
  • Poles: Expected to arrive in the new year (early 2027).
  • Note: The County will provide a finalized schedule once BGE completes their planning.

The county has shared the official streetlight layout and zoning maps for both sections. These maps highlight the precise placements and coverage of the new fixtures. You can click on either map below to open the high-resolution version in a new tab.
