In December 2025, we announced the SCCA Board of Directors voted to pursue designation as a Special Community Benefits District (SCBD). This would allow Anne Arundel County, through the property tax process, to establish separate funding dedicated exclusively to maintaining the 45 acres of common property and other assets described as follows:

The purposes of the Special Community Benefit District are:

  1. 1) To maintain and improve community-owned real and personal property, including open space, recreation property, and entry signs for Sections 1 and 2 of the community.
  2. 2) To pay administrative expenses to accomplish this purpose, including repayment of any loan and interest thereon, and payment of insurance, legal, utility, and administrative expenses.

This fund would be administered by the SCCA. Membership in the association would continue to be voluntary and open to all residents.

We are pursuing this because currently only about 70 percent of SCCA homeowners pay annual dues, while community income does not keep pace with expenses. An SCBD would ensure all homes contribute and provide the financial stability needed to address maintenance and improvements.

On July 24, Anne Arundel County staff confirmed our eligibility and approved the wording of our petition. In order to obtain approval for the SCBD, our next steps are:

  1. 1) obtain signatures from two-thirds of SCCA homeowners
  2. 2) submit the signed petitions to the County for review.

Beginning in September, we will share more information on our website and in the annual newsletter, with an informational Q&A planned for our October annual meeting. We are committed to keeping the community informed and being open and transparent throughout the process.
