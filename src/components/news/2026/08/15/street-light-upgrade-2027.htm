The Board of Directors contacted the County's Traffic Engineering Division to request replacement of the original street lights in our neighborhood which are 45 years old and in poor condition. The senior engineer in charge of the County's street lights responded immediately and agreed to replace them. The lights in the SCHOA part of Shipley's were already replaced and our new lights will be like those. The new light fixtures and poles will improve the appearance of the neighborhood. The fixtures are brighter LED lights which will improve safety.

We are hopeful the street light replacement project will begin in the Spring of 2027. The senior engineer explained the funding for this project comes from two different sources and, consequently, the upgrade will take place in stages; the fixtures will be replaced first and then the poles. Your Board of Directors is working hard to improve the neighborhood!
