A friendly reminder: all work on community-owned common grounds must be approved by the SCCA Board before it is undertaken.

While we deeply appreciate the spirit of volunteerism, this policy is essential for several reasons. First and foremost is safety and liability; uncoordinated work can create hazards and expose our community to legal and financial risks. Additionally, any modifications to common areas must comply with county zoning laws and align with the community's long-term landscaping and maintenance plans.

Under SCCA bylaws Article IV, Sections 1 & 4, Association-owned facilities and common properties are reserved strictly for the managed use and enjoyment of members in good standing under rules established by the Association. Individual homeowners do not have individual property rights to alter, landscape, or build on common property.
