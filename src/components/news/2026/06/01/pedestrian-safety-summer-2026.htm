We've noticed an increase in residents walking in the street, especially during evenings and on weekends. While we love to see our neighbors out and about, this can create hazardous situations for both pedestrians and drivers.

For everyone's safety, we strongly urge all residents to use sidewalks whenever they are available. When walking on a street without a sidewalk, please walk facing traffic and stay as far to the side of the road as possible. Let's all do our part to keep Shipley's Choice a safe community for everyone.
