Get Involved with The SCCA

­

September 3, 2026

­

The Shipley's Choice Community Association (SCCA) Board of Directors is pleased to see so much interest and engagement in our community. We hear you – and we want you to be involved.


Now is your opportunity to help shape the future of Shipley's Choice and continue making it a great place to live. We currently have three openings on the Board of Directors: Treasurer and two Director positions.


Any resident of Sections 1 and 2 who is in good standing (meaning annual dues are current) may be nominated or may nominate themselves. If you've been following the recent conversations on Facebook and have been thinking about getting involved, now is the time to step forward.


We also want to clarify one point: suggestions that the SCCA will be taken over by Anne Arundel County are without merit. The current Board openings are the result of normal organizational turnover. One longtime Director passed away earlier this year, while the Treasurer and another Director are completing their two-year terms and will be retiring from the Board.


We encourage interested residents to consider serving and bringing their ideas, experience, and perspective to the Board. Your involvement can make a difference! To be considered for a Board position, please send your nomination or self-nomination to shipleyschoice.scca@gmail.com.


We look forward to hearing from you!

Neighborhood Street Light Upgrade

­

August 15, 2026

­

The Board of Directors contacted the County's Traffic Engineering Division to request replacement of the original street lights in our neighborhood which are 45 years old and in poor condition. The senior engineer in charge of the County's street lights responded immediately and agreed to replace them. The lights in the SCHOA part of Shipley's were already replaced and our new lights will be like those. The new light fixtures and poles will improve the appearance of the neighborhood. The fixtures are brighter LED lights which will improve safety.

We are hopeful the street light replacement project will begin in the Spring of 2027. The senior engineer explained the funding for this project comes from two different sources and, consequently, the upgrade will take place in stages; the fixtures will be replaced first and then the poles. Your Board of Directors is working hard to improve the neighborhood!

Designating Shipley's Choice Community Association as a Special Community Benefits District (SCBD) 

­

August 13, 2026

­

In December 2025, we announced the SCCA Board of Directors voted to pursue designation as a Special Community Benefits District (SCBD). This would allow Anne Arundel County, through the property tax process, to establish separate funding dedicated exclusively to maintaining the 45 acres of common property and other assets described as follows:


The purposes of the Special Community Benefit District are:


  1. To maintain and improve community-owned real and personal property, including open space, recreation property, and entry signs for Sections 1 and 2 of the community.
  2. To pay administrative expenses to accomplish this purpose, including repayment of any loan and interest thereon, and payment of insurance, legal, utility, and administrative expenses.


This fund would be administered by the SCCA. Membership in the association would continue to be voluntary and open to all residents.


We are pursuing this because currently only about 70 percent of SCCA homeowners pay annual dues, while community income does not keep pace with expenses. An SCBD would ensure all homes contribute and provide the financial stability needed to address maintenance and improvements.


On July 24, Anne Arundel County staff confirmed our eligibility and approved the wording of our petition. In order to obtain approval for the SCBD, our next steps are:


  1. Obtain signatures from two-thirds of SCCA homeowners
  2. Submit the signed petitions to the County for review.


Beginning in September, we will share more information on our website and in the annual newsletter, with an informational Q&A planned for our October annual meeting. We are committed to keeping the community informed and being open and transparent throughout the process.


More information to come!

Community News & Upcoming Events

­

As we continue working to keep our community informed, we want to clarify where residents can find official information from the SCCA Board.


Residents may see information or discussions about SCCA matters shared through social media groups, community forums, or other channels. These spaces can provide areas for neighbors for share ideas and perspectives; however, information shared in these forums is not necessarily reviewed, approved, or endorsed by the SCCA Board. 


For official updates regarding the SCCA we encourage residents to rely on communications issued directly by the SCCA board and on the official SCCA website. 


We will continue working to provide regular updates and welcome questions, ideas, and suggestions. Our goal is not to limit community discussion, but to make sure residents have access – and know where to find – accurate information on what's happening in the SCCA. 


The Next SCCA Annual Membership Meeting will be held in early October. More specific details will be shared soon.
